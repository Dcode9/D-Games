Echo Drift

Drift around, grab the glowing echoes before they fade, and steer clear of the red rings. New here? Do the tutorial.

  • Drag the stick (or WASD / arrows) to move. Hold BOOST (Space) to dash.
  • Green echoes fade fast. Grab them to build a CHAIN and refill the field.
  • Red rings drain the field. A dashed ring is a warning: it turns live in a moment.
  • Every 5 chain starts a new wave. Echoes fade faster and rings drift. Field empty = signal lost.
