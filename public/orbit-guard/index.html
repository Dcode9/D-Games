Orbit Guard

Spin the shield around the core and bat away incoming rocks. New here? The tutorial walks through each button.

  • Hold LEFT or RIGHT to spin the shield. You can also drag around the core.
  • FLIP swings the shield to the opposite side. It needs a moment to recharge.
  • A rock that touches the core costs 20 integrity. Five hits and the core is lost.
  • Rock colors start vivid and get dustier each wave, and they come faster.
Keys: arrows or A/D spin, Space flip, P pause.
