Neon Snake

Steer, eat, grow. New here? The tutorial walks through each button.

  • Press the arrow keys, swipe on the board, or use the four keys below to turn.
  • Red apples add 10. Gold stars add 50. Purple scissors add 25 and trim your tail.
  • Walls and your own tail end the run. You cannot reverse straight back.
  • The snake speeds up as you score, and its colors start bright and fade by level.
Keys: arrows or WASD turn, P pause.
