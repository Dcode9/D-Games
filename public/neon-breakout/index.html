Neon Breakout

Bounce the ball off your paddle and clear every brick. New here? The tutorial walks through each button.

  • ◀ ▶ slide the paddle. You can also drag on the board.
  • LAUNCH sends the ball up. Hit it with the paddle edge to steer.
  • Capsules: M multiball, W wide paddle, Z lasers (LAUNCH fires), S shield.
  • Colors start vivid and get dustier every level. Tough bricks take two hits.
Keys: arrows or A/D move, Space launch or fire, P pause.
