Neon Galaxy

Your ship fires on its own. Fly, dodge, and clear the sectors. New here? The tutorial walks through each button.

  • Hold the arrow keys to fly. You can also drag on the board.
  • Auto-fire is always on. Rams cost 30 shield, bullets cost 15.
  • Capsules: G upgrades your gun for a while, S restores shield.
  • Enemy colors start vivid and get dustier every sector. Scouts, interceptors, heavies.
Keys: arrows or WASD fly, P pause.
