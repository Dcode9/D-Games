3XO

Three players share one board. Be first to line up four. New here? The tutorial walks through it.

  • Pass the device. Turns go olive triangle, blue X, brick O.
  • Tap a free square to place your mark.
  • Four in a row wins: across, down or diagonal.
  • GUIDE lights the square that wins, blocks, or builds your line.
  • Colors start vivid and fade as the board fills.
UNDO takes back the last move. Keys: Z undo, N new match, P pause.
