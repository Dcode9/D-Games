Neon Tetris

Classic falling blocks. Complete rows to clear them. New here? The tutorial walks through every button.

  • ◀ ▶ move. ↻ rotates. ▼ drops faster. HARD DROP slams to the outline.
  • HOLD stores a piece for later. One swap per piece.
  • The outline shows where the piece will land (GUIDE).
  • Colors start bright and get dustier each level. Speed goes up every 10 lines.
Keys: arrows or A/D move, W/Z rotate, S soft drop, Space hard drop, C hold, P pause.
