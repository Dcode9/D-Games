Cyber Road

Hold the line down a procedural highway and weave around the blocks. New here? The tutorial walks through each button.

  • Hold ◀ or ▶ to steer. You can also drag anywhere on the road.
  • Hold BRAKE to slow down and buy time. Brakes cost distance, not lives.
  • The opening stretch is clear. Blocks get denser the further you go.
  • Colors start vivid and get dustier as you go. The guide ring marks the block to avoid.
Keys: arrows or A/D steer, S or Down brake, P pause.
