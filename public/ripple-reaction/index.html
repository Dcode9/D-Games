Ripple Reaction

Drop ripples to catch the teal nodes. Keep the red ones out of the ring. New here? The tutorial walks through the controls.

  • Tap anywhere on the field to drop a ripple. It grows and pulls nearby teal nodes in.
  • AUTO DROP puts a ripple on the best spot for you (Space).
  • Catch teal nodes back to back to raise the combo, up to x12.
  • A red node caught by a ripple costs a life and resets the combo. Three lives.
  • Colors start vivid and fade as the level climbs.
Keys: Space auto drop, P pause. Tap or click to place.
